Windows 键盘效率工具Keyboard shortcuts for Windows

让 CapsLock 多帮你做点事。Put your CapsLock key to work.

capslock_p2 让你按住 CapsLock 就能移动光标、选中文字，也能快速打开程序、找文件、记笔记和查单词。先记住几个常用键，用到什么再学什么。With capslock_p2, hold CapsLock to move the cursor and select text, or quickly open apps, find files, keep notes and look up words. Start with a few shortcuts and learn the rest as you need them.

先记住这个按法How to press a shortcut

看到 CapsLock + Q，就是按住 CapsLock，再按一下 Q，然后松开。松开后照常打字。单独短按 CapsLock，默认切换大小写；也可以在设置中改成其他动作。When you see CapsLock + Q, hold CapsLock, tap Q, then release. Type normally once you let go. A quick tap of CapsLock toggles capital letters by default; you can change this in Settings.

第一次用，先试这三步Try these three steps first

  1. 打开程序Launch the app

    安装后打开 capslock_p2，看到任务栏右下角的程序图标，就可以开始用了。首次安装启动时，这份使用介绍会自动打开一次；之后按 CapsLock + F1 可再次打开。Open capslock_p2 after installing it. Its icon in the Windows system tray means it is running. On the first launch after installation, this guide opens once automatically; press CapsLock + F1 to open it again later.

  2. 试着移动光标Move the cursor

    打开记事本，输入一行字。按住 CapsLock，再按 S 或 F，光标就会向左或向右移动。Open Notepad and type a line. Hold CapsLock and press S or F to move left or right.

  3. 看看设置Explore Settings

    按 CapsLock + F12 打开设置，按自己的习惯调整。基础快捷键可以直接用；翻译和 AI 需要先填写服务信息。Press CapsLock + F12 to open Settings and make it your own. Basic shortcuts work straight away; online translation and AI need service details first.

点击文本框后，按住 CapsLock 再按 S / F 移动光标，按 J / L 选择文字。你也可以直接改写这段示例。

Click the text box, then hold CapsLock and press S / F to move the cursor, or J / L to select text. You can edit the sample too.

日常会用到的几个工具Tools for everyday tasks

下面列的是默认按键和命令，可以在设置中修改。These are the default shortcuts and commands. You can change them in Settings.

CapsLock + Q

打开程序、搜索网页Open apps and search the web

在 qbar 输入程序名，用 ↑ ↓ 选择，回车打开。也可用缩写：wyy 找“网易云音乐”，vs 找 Visual Studio Code。输入 bing 天气 搜索网页。Type an app name in qbar, choose a result with ↑ ↓, and press Enter. Try initials too: wyy finds NetEase Cloud Music, and vs finds Visual Studio Code. Type bing weather to search the web.

CapsLock + Q

找电脑里的文件Find files on your PC

在 qbar 输入 e、everything、find 或 f 打开搜索页；e 报告 直接搜索“报告”。左侧可按类型筛选，输入 *.pdf 找 PDF；右键可复制文件或在文件夹中显示。点「固定」可让窗口保持显示，点「独立窗口」可使用任务栏和系统标题栏。Type e, everything, find or f in qbar to open file search; e report searches for “report”. Filter by type, use *.pdf to find PDFs, or right-click to copy a file or show it in its folder. Pin keeps the window visible; Independent window adds a taskbar button and system title bar.

CapsLock + T

查单词、翻译选中文字Look up words and translate text

选中文字后按快捷键：词典收录的英文单词显示释义，其他文字进入翻译；没选文字时可直接输入。可固定翻译成一种语言，或设两种语言互译，在面板中交换方向。在线翻译需要先在设置中填写服务信息。Select text and press the shortcut: an English word in the dictionary shows its definition; other text opens in Translate. With nothing selected, you can type your own text. Translate into one language or swap between two. Set up a provider in Settings for online translation.

CapsLock + F7

问 AI，也可以接着追问Ask AI and follow up

按快捷键打开聊天。也可在 qbar 输入 ai 帮我写一封请假邮件，回车开始对话；只输入 ai 或 q 可打开空白聊天。可以追问，或从侧栏继续历史会话，也可重命名、置顶和删除。第一次使用，先在设置的「LLM」中填写服务信息。Press the shortcut to open chat. You can also type ai Help me write a leave request in qbar and press Enter, or use ai or q for a blank chat. Follow up or continue a conversation from the sidebar; you can also rename, pin or delete it. Enter your provider details under LLM in Settings before your first question.

CapsLock + F2

记录和查找便签Keep notes at hand

按快捷键打开笔记。也可在 qbar 输入 n、note、w 或 write 打开笔记；后面跟文字即可搜索。右下角按钮新增，点击标题编辑，Ctrl + S 保存。可写标题、列表和表格，也可粘贴图片；卡片右键可复制、粘贴、置顶或删除。Press the shortcut to open notes. You can also type n, note, w or write in qbar; text after the command searches your notes. Use the bottom-right button to add a note, click its title to edit, and press Ctrl + S to save. Add headings, lists, tables or pasted images; right-click a card to copy, paste, pin or delete it.

CapsLock + C / CapsLock + V

留住两份常用内容Keep two things ready to paste

用 CapsLock + C 复制、CapsLock + V 粘贴第一份内容。两边都多按住一个左 Alt，就是第二份；例如 CapsLock + LAlt + C 复制第二份。平常的 Ctrl + V 用来粘贴系统剪贴板。Use CapsLock + C to copy and CapsLock + V to paste from the first clipboard. Hold left Alt as well for the second, for example CapsLock + LAlt + C to copy. Your usual Ctrl + V pastes from the system clipboard.

CapsLock + Z

找回之前复制的内容Find something you copied earlier

打开剪贴板历史，或在 qbar 输入 cv；cv 关键词 可以直接搜索。支持文本、图片和文件，单击复制、双击粘贴到之前的窗口。可以收藏、添加备注或按日期筛选；记录开关和保存天数在设置的「qbar」中调整。Open clipboard history, or type cv in qbar; cv keyword searches it directly. Find text, images and files, click to copy, or double-click to paste into your previous window. Add favorites or notes, filter by date, and adjust recording and retention under qbar in Settings.

CapsLock + Tab

少打几遍重复的话Expand text you type often

在设置的「Tab 替换」中添加一个缩写和完整内容，例如把 addr 设为常用地址。以后输入 addr，再按 CapsLock + Tab，就能替换成完整地址。Add an abbreviation and its full text under Tab replacements in Settings, for example addr for your address. Type addr, then press CapsLock + Tab to expand it.

CapsLock + F12

想调整？打开设置就好Make it yours in Settings

程序运行时，按住 CapsLock，再按 F12，即可打开设置中心。也可以右键任务栏右下角的程序图标，选择「设置」。While the app is running, hold CapsLock and press F12 to open Settings. You can also right-click its system tray icon and choose Settings.

  • 改按键：在「快捷键」顶部选择全局或某个应用。应用里没改的按键沿用全局设置。Change shortcuts: choose Global or an app at the top of Shortcuts. Unchanged app shortcuts use the global settings.
  • 自定义快捷键：录制按键后，可选发送另一组按键、调用自带功能、保留原功能或禁用。Customize shortcuts: record a key combination, then send different keys, choose a built-in action, keep the original behavior or disable it.
  • 启用翻译和 AI：在「LLM」中填写服务商提供的地址、密钥和模型；在「翻译」中选择语言，也可改用有道或火山引擎。Set up translation and AI: enter your provider's address, key and model under LLM. Choose languages under Translate, where you can also set up Youdao or Volcengine.
  • 设置常用工具：在「Tab 替换」添加常用文字，在「qbar」管理网址、程序和工具，在「窗口绑定」给窗口分配数字键。Set up your tools: add common text under Tab replacements, manage websites, apps and tools under qbar, and assign number keys under Window bindings.

修改后记得点「保存」。大多数设置会立即生效；开机自启动也可以在托盘右键菜单中开启。Click Save when you are done. Most changes take effect immediately. You can also enable launch at startup from the tray menu.

第一次用，可能会想问A few things you might wonder

需要先设置才能用吗？Do I need to configure anything first?

移动光标、选择文字、独立剪贴板、剪贴板历史、本地词典和笔记可以直接用。在线翻译和 AI 需要你自己的服务信息，服务商可能收费。按 CapsLock + F12 就能开始设置。Cursor movement, text selection, the extra clipboards, clipboard history, local dictionary and notes work straight away. Online translation and AI need your own provider details, and the provider may charge for use. Press CapsLock + F12 to set them up.

文件搜索为什么弹出管理员提示？Why does file search ask for administrator permission?

文件搜索使用 Everything。程序会优先尝试使用已经运行的 Everything；需要启动内置版本时，可能出现管理员确认，建立索引也需要一点时间。File search uses Everything. The app first tries an already running copy. Starting the bundled copy may ask for administrator permission, and building the file index can take a little time.

快捷键没反应，先看哪里？What if a shortcut does nothing?

先确认程序正在运行，任务栏右下角有它的图标。按组合键时要一直按住 CapsLock。部分笔记本的 F1–F12 默认是音量或亮度键，需要同时按 Fn，或开启 Fn Lock。Check that the app is running and its tray icon is visible. Keep CapsLock held while pressing the other key. On some laptops, F1–F12 control volume or brightness by default; hold Fn too, or enable Fn Lock.

下载的是源码，怎么启动？How do I run the source version?

安装 AutoHotkey v2 后，双击 capslock_p2.ahk。如果工具面板提示缺少 WebView2，请安装 Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime。Install AutoHotkey v2, then double-click capslock_p2.ahk. If a tool panel reports that WebView2 is missing, install the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime.

capslock_p2 是基于 Capslock+ 的 AutoHotkey v2 重写项目，沿用它的快捷键思路，并加入翻译、AI、笔记等工具。以 GPL v2 许可开源。capslock_p2 is an AutoHotkey v2 rewrite of Capslock+, keeping its shortcut approach and adding tools such as translation, AI and notes. Open source under GPL v2.
